# Hosted with harvest.je

If you want the simplest and most elegant path, use [harvest.je](https://harvest.je).

`harvest.je` is **fully managed hosting** for Harvest. The technical work is handled by the people who built Harvest, including platform setup, upgrades, monitoring, backups, and operational support.

If your only goal is to get Harvest deployed and running quickly with the least technical risk, this is the recommended route.

## Best for

- agri-businesses that want to go live quickly
- teams without internal infrastructure or DevOps capacity
- operators who want technical support from the Harvest builders

## What is managed for you

With `harvest.je`, you do **not** need to run your own infrastructure or deployment tooling. The hosting team handles:

- infrastructure setup and production rollout
- release and upgrade operations
- monitoring, incident response, and support handoff
- backup routines and operational maintenance

## Typical setup steps

1. Create your hosted account.
2. Complete your business details and setup initial team access.
3. Confirm your onboarding with the Harvest team.
4. Validate your production domain, team logins, and core workflows.
5. Start running supplier ordering, stock, and daily operations in Harvest.

## Best-practice checkpoints before go-live

- confirm who in your team owns day-to-day admin access
- complete a short end-to-end workflow test (supplier → stock → order)

If your team needs to run Harvest on your own servers, use the technical guide: [Self-hosted with Kamal](/guides/getting-started/self-hosted-with-kamal).
