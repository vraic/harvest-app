# Retention events and holds

Use the **Data Retention Events** index to audit what happened and why.

## Manual hold use case

Apply a hold when a record must be retained longer than normal policy.

## What to review regularly

- records skipped due to hold
- anonymisation and purge events
- unusual spikes in retention activity
